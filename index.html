The Coaching Method
A Structured Four-Step Academic Process

Every session follows an educationally grounded method — guiding your child to master the material with lasting clarity and purpose.

1

Student Submits the Question

Your child selects their grade and subject, then types their homework question. The Homework Coach analyzes what academic skill is being tested and adjusts its approach to guide step-by-step.

2

The Task Is Broken Into Structured Steps

Rather than presenting one overwhelming question, The Homework Coach immediately restructures the problem into smaller, manageable parts — each one deliberately structured to deepen comprehension at every stage.

3

Guided Prompts Develop Critical Thinking

The Homework Coach responds exclusively with targeted questions and structured hints — prompting your child to analyze, reason, and arrive at each answer through their own academic thinking.

4

Full Understanding Is Achieved

With guided support through each structured step, your child works through the complete assignment until the concept is fully understood — and measurable academic progress becomes evident with every session.

Student
What is 25% of 80?
The Homework Coach
Here's a helpful trick: 25% is the same as one-fourth — like cutting something into 4 equal pieces. So finding 25% of 80 is the same as dividing 80 into 4 equal groups. What is 80 ÷ 4?
Student
20
The Homework Coach
Perfect! You just solved it — 25% of 80 is 20. You used the fraction shortcut efficiently. 🎉

Built for ADHD Learners

Structured. Patient. One step at a time.

Traditional homework help either gives instant answers or provides long, confusing explanations. The Homework Coach does the opposite — breaking every problem into clear micro-steps, celebrating small wins, and never rushing. Ollie's responses are short, direct, and structured exactly how ADHD brains learn best.

One Micro-Step at a Time

Ollie never presents the full problem at once. Every assignment is broken into the smallest possible step — one at a time — so ADHD students never feel overwhelmed or lost.

⏸️

Never Frustrated, Never Rushed

Consistent, calm support every single time. If a student needs the same concept explained five different ways — Ollie finds five different ways. No sighing. No judgment. Ever.

Every Ollie response is 2-3 short lines maximum. No walls of text. No information overload. Just the next small step — perfectly sized for an ADHD attention span.

🔄

No Judgment for Lost Focus

What Parents Ask Before Starting

Will The Homework Coach give my child the answers?

Never. Ollie guides your child through their own reasoning process using structured questions and hints. Providing direct answers is a deliberate design decision that protects academic development.

Is this safe for my child to use independently?

Yes. The Homework Coach responds exclusively to academic, age-appropriate homework questions. No personal conversations, social topics, or non-academic content — regardless of what is submitted.

Which subjects and grades are supported?

Math, Reading, Writing, Science, and Social Studies — for students in 3rd through 9th grade. The system automatically adjusts complexity based on the grade selected.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts, no commitments, no cancellation fees. Cancel directly from your account settings whenever you choose.
